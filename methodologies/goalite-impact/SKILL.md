# IMPACT Framework

Turn an ambition into a funded, measurable plan with the IMPACT Framework — conducted step by step by AI, certified by the method's owner.

The IMPACT Framework walks you from a raw ambition to a working, reviewable plan across six stages: Identify, Motivate, Plan, Act, Check, Transform. Each stage is conducted as a guided working session — the AI probes vague answers, pushes for specifics, and captures structured decisions you can revisit — and each stage produces its own deliverable, composed into a final certified plan.

## Journey
- Identify
- Motivate
- Plan: Break the prioritised goal into milestones, concrete steps, and supporting habits. The build-plan step drafts a structured plan for review; when you confirm it, the platform creates the related work items and can render the confirmed plan as a document. Where the goal's measure of success is something you can track as a number over time (revenue, a rate, a count), set it up as a tracked metric linked to the goal so progress can be reviewed later; keep a one-time outcome as a milestone with a target date.
- Act
- Check: Reflect on real progress and refine the plan. Review the goal's tracked measures of success — the latest readings against their target band — alongside completed steps and habit streaks, and adjust the plan where lived experience suggests. Record fresh readings for any tracked measure before reflecting, so the review is judged on current evidence.
- Transform
